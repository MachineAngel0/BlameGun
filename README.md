Gravity's Brutal is a first person shooter where the player can manipulate their own velocity and gravity to defeat unique and tricky enemies. The player gets 4 unique guns as well as unique movement abilities. There are three levels with a boss at the final level.
Made in Unreal Engine using primarly c++. 
The game is free, it's hosted on [itch.io](https://machineangel0.itch.io/gravitys-brutal), or a zip can be found in releases.
